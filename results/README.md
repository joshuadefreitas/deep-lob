# Results provenance

The five legacy experiment directories below were committed in November 2025,
before the validation study. Their `summary.json` files are preserved evidence
of pre-study behaviour and must not be regenerated or overwritten.

Each legacy summary embeds the configuration that actually produced it. The
recorded `classification_metrics.accuracy` was computed by `evaluate_model()`
over every generated window, including windows used to train the model. These
figures are therefore training-set accuracy, not held-out accuracy.

| Existing directory | Config recorded in `summary.json` | Window | Horizon | Recorded accuracy |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `baseline_a/` | `h20_L100` | 100 | 20 | 0.7441098135627945 |
| `h10_L200/` | `h10_L200` | 200 | 10 | 0.6071801294093091 |
| `h10_L50/` | `h10_L50` | 50 | 10 | 0.5992714025500910 |
| `h20_L100/` | `h20_L100` | 100 | 20 | 0.7488219627125589 |
| `h5_L100/` | `h5_L100` | 100 | 5 | 0.5743464052287581 |

`study/` is not a legacy single-config experiment directory. It contains the
180-run validation study generated by `run_study.py` in August 2026. Its
`summary.json` distinguishes validation-only `val_acc` from all-window
`full_acc`.
